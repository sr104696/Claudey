# 01 — Tangential & orthogonal sector map (recall expansion)

The radar's segments today: fund, litigation_finance, fintech, legal_ai, policy_research, trading,
credit_intelligence, public, crypto, research_vendor, exchange, ai_eval, trade_assoc,
prediction_markets, bank, ai_lab, sro, investigative_fund. This doc lists sectors that are
*superficially adjacent or orthogonal* but produce the same job shape from CLAUDE.md: bounded
questions, primary-source investigation, synthesis, decision-useful writing, advisory output,
no court time / no quota / no spreadsheet production.

For each: why it maps, named firm pool (→ new `seeds/companies.csv` rows), and how to find their
boards with existing machinery (Phase 2 ATS detection handles the rest).

## A. Disputes analytics / expert-witness & damages consulting
**Why:** literally "litigation is the domain" + analytic synthesis; hires JDs and PhD-economists
as associates; clerkships strongly preferred. Closest isomorphic family to litigation finance and
almost entirely off-radar.
**Firms:** Analysis Group, Cornerstone Research, Compass Lexecon, Charles River Associates,
Prometheus Consulting, FTI Consulting, Guidehouse, Berkeley Research Group,
Houlihan Lokey (valuation/disputes advisory), Kroll (Duff & Phelps disputes arm), PwC Forensics,
EY-Parthenon (litigation services), NERA Economic Consulting, Resource Systems Group.
**Titles to match:** "Associate (Litigation Support/Economic Consulting)", "Forensic Analyst",
"Damages Associate", "Investigations Associate".
**Boards:** most use Greenhouse/Workday/Lever — detectable. BRG/Nera on Workday tenants.

## B. Geopolitical / country-risk / integrity due diligence
**Why:** investigate → write decision-useful memo for clients; regulatory-subject-matter heavy;
pays well at senior analyst tiers; ex-clerks common hires.
**Firms:** Eurasia Group (now EIG, a Marsh McLennan company), Control Risks, S-RM, Kroll
(Integrity Services — also in A), Nardello & Co., StoneTurn, Exiger, LSEG/Refinitiv World-Check
(legal-intelligence analyst seats), Dow Jones Risk & Compliance, ComplyAdvantage, Castellum,
Verisk Maplecroft.
**Titles:** "Political Risk Analyst", "Country Risk Associate", "Integrity Diligence Associate",
"OSINT Analyst (Legal)", "Compliance Advisory Manager".
**Note:** Jane's Intelligence (janes.com) hires research analysts with law backgrounds.

## C. Competitive intelligence & knowledge management inside law firms & banks
**Why:** pure primary-source research + synthesis for decision-makers; JD-preferred at "Practice
Attorney / Knowledge Management Lawyer" level; exists at every Am Law 50 firm — a huge untapped
board population because these roles sit *inside law firms* which the current rules treat as
associate-excluded. The KM/CI seats are not associate seats; they should be a whitelist carve-out.
**Firms (KM/CI desks):** Kirkland (he knows it), Skadden, Sullivan & Cromwell, Wachtell, Simpson
Thacher, Gibson Dunn ("Practice Attorney" network), Paul Weiss, Davis Polk, Cravath, plus banks:
Goldman Sachs Knowledge Gateway, JPMorgan Global Research, BofA Research. For additional firms,
mine the SCIP (Strategic & Competitive Intelligence Professionals) community directory.
**Titles:** "Knowledge Management Lawyer", "Practice Attorney", "Research Attorney", "Practice
Support Attorney", "Market Intelligence Analyst", "Competitive Intelligence Associate".
**Scoring change needed:** exempt KM/CI titles from the "law-firm associate" poor-match rule when
title lacks "associate attorney" and mentions research/knowledge/practice support.

## D. Insurance coverage / specialty lines & reinsurance
**Why:** coverage disputes = litigation-domain reading+writing without court time; "Claims
Counsel"/"Coverage Counsel" is a known JD landing spot; brokers (Guy Carpenter, Marsh, Aon,
Willis Towers Watson) hire legal-analytic analysts; Bermuda/NYC hubs.
**Firms:** Chubb (has NYC legal analyst postings), AIG, Hartford, Travelers, AXIS Capital,
RenaissanceRe, Markel, Hiscox NA, Arch, CNA, Beazley, QBE NA; brokers: Marsh, Aon, WTW, Guy
Carpenter, Gallagher, Howden; TPA/specialty: Sedgwick (huge claims-legal org), Crawford & Co.
**Titles:** "Coverage Counsel", "Claims Counsel", "Complex Claims Analyst", "Subrogation Attorney".
**Boards:** mostly Workday/iCIMS — needs the Workday-sweep fix from the prior review; add here too.

## E. Sanctions, export control & trade compliance advisory
**Why:** OFAC/BIS rule interpretation for decisions; regulatory-research DNA; boutiques staffed by
ex-Treasury lawyers; overlaps his MS exam-support work.
**Firms:** Deloitte Forensic, PwC Sanctions,
EY SASS, Kroll (again), StoneTurn (again). Institutions whose
job pages matter more: OFAC itself (Treasury — USAJobs channel), BIS (Commerce), ITC usitc.gov
(hearings attorneys!), FINRA/OCC (partially covered).
**Titles:** "Export Control Analyst", "Trade Compliance Counsel", "Sanctions Advisory Associate",
"International Trade Attorney".

## F. Arbitration & dispute-resolution institutions
**Why:** case administration + merits analysis; clerkship-valued; NYC is ICDR HQ.
**Orgs:** ICC Court (Paris/NY), ICDR/AAA (adr.org — uses iCIMS?), WIPO (Geneva, remote-unlikely),
JAMS (neutral panel jobs), CPR Institute (policy counsel posts), Permanent Court of Arbitration?
skip NY relevance. Also LCIA London → outside-location table.
**Titles:** "Case Manager (Arbitration)", "Legal Counsel, ICDR", "Program Counsel, CPR".

## G. Public-sector beyond current portals (the biggest blind spot)
Already: NYDFS, NY AG, statejobs.ny.gov, NY Fed, FINRA, (USAJobs gated on key). Add:
- **Federal Reserve Board + 11 other district banks** (federalreserve.org/careers public feed;
  Philadelphia/Richmond/Chicago/Kansas City post "Legal Division" and "Supervision" analysts;
  KC Fed has a well-known economics+law fellow pipeline).
- **SEC Division of Corporation Finance / FinCEN / FHFA Ombudsman / CFPB (careers page is static
  HTML even when USAJobs API needs key)**; OCC's own careers page (occ.gov) posts directly.
- **FDIC** careers (fdic.gov — static listings, Regional Attorney offices, complex litigation unit).
- **USCourts**: uscourts.gov "Careers" links per-district HTML; SDNY/EDNY/SDNJ bankruptcy chambers
  clerk-to-judge postings (matches the D.N.J. bankruptcy exposure story exactly); USCIT (trade
  court clerks — ties to E/ITC).
- **NY county DA/agencies**: NYC Jobs portal (nyc.gov/portal careers — SuccessFactors-based,
  scrapeable), Manhattan DA Appeals Unit (hires Penn-law-tier clerks), NY Appellate Divisions.
- **Congressional**: House/Senate Office of General Counsel, Senate Banking Committee majority/minority
  counsel (legislative-aid salary but elite signal; via congress.gov? hiring pages are per-office
  HTML — low yield, note only).
- **State AG consortium outside NY**: NJ AG (already adjacent via bankruptcy court), CT/MA financial
  regulation units — interagency secondments make lateral hires.

## H. Prediction/forecasting & evaluation orgs (extends ai_eval/prediction_markets)
**Why:** bounded questions, written probabilistic conclusions, pay rising fast.
**Orgs:** Metaculus (remote), Good Judgment Inc. (NYC-adjacent, forecasting + consulting memos),
FutureSearch (remote researcher network). AI-evals orgs with policy-research seats:
METR and Apollo Research (mostly remote/UK — outside-location table unless remote-eligible).
**Titles:** "Forecasting Consultant", "Research Analyst (Geopolitical Forecasting)", "Eval Researcher (Policy)".

## I. Legal-market data & media (extends research_vendor)
Already: Debtwire, Octus, 9fin, Fitch. Add: Law360/Reuters Legal (hires "Analyst/Reporter, Bankruptcy &
Restructuring" — exact rubric fit: reads primary dockets, writes decision-useful briefs).
Bloomberg Law remains behind the same Cloudflare wall already logged in Known Limits.
TRAC (Syracuse — immigration FOIA data, research associate roles), Ballotpedia (research),
Justia (analyst content), CourtListener/Free Law Project (legal-data non-profit, remote,
hires "Legal Fellow/Data Librarian").
Also credit-media: PitchBook (NYC — "Bankruptcy & Restructuring Analyst" role class),
S&P LCD / Moody's LCD (loan-leverage commentary teams hire JD-econ profiles), Mergermarket/Tortoise
Media (Milken) — M&A research writers.

## J. Faith-based/human-rights/legal aid with decision-writing? 
Deliberately excluded: mission-fit but pay floor ($150K base rule) fails almost universally.
Skip this whole branch; noted so future runs don't spend request budget here.

## K. Sports/entertainment/esports integrity & gaming compliance (cheap to test, likely misses)
**Why listed:** "investigate primary sources → written finding" is literally sports wagering
integrity analyst work (Sportradar US, Genius Sports, Integrity Alliance; DraftKings/FanDuel
compliance desks in NYC). Pay often clears $150K at senior. Low priority but near-zero pull cost.
